How does SLP scale with customer growth?

Program growth is the goal — but it is also the event that exposes every structural weakness in a medical logistics operation. Strategic Lab Partners is built on a scalable infrastructure model that absorbs volume increases without requiring clients to renegotiate contracts, onboard new vendors, or rebuild operational processes, while SLP CONNECT maintains the same governance and visibility regardless of order volume.

SLP scales by expanding ship stations, staffing, and supporting infrastructure as your program grows. Because our processes, systems, and quality controls are standardized, we can increase throughput without sacrificing accuracy, visibility, or compliance.

Practical applications:

  • Increasing kit production capacity by adding assembly shifts within SLP's existing regulated facility footprint.
  • Expanding outbound fulfillment throughput through carrier network surge agreements pre-negotiated by SLP.
  • Onboarding new kit SKUs or program variants in SLP CONNECT without disrupting existing program operations.
  • Adding new sites or geographies to an existing program with configuration changes rather than operational rebuilds.
  • Maintaining SLP CONNECT performance and data integrity at scale without system re-implementation.

During onboarding, we model growth scenarios—for example, increasing from 1,300 kits per week to 2,500 kits per week over a 12‑month period. This allows us to align staffing, equipment, inventory strategy, and carrier capacity with your projected demand.

Our goal is to ensure that your program can scale smoothly, without bottlenecks or operational surprises, while maintaining the same level of accuracy and reliability at every stage.

What this prevents: delays, loss without defensible history, inventory gaps, and after-the-fact reconciliation.

SLP Advantage: You get a partner that plans for growth instead of reacting to it—ensuring your program scales predictably and reliably.

If your program is decentralized or regulated and you're experiencing avoidable disruption, SLP can help you map failure points and implement a more governable approach.
