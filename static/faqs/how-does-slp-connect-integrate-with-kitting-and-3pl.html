How does SLP CONNECT™ integrate with kitting and 3PL?

SLP CONNECT™ integrates directly with SLP’s kitting and 3PL operations, creating a unified system that connects orders, inventory, and deployment workflows into a single digital environment. This ensures every kit, component, and shipment is tracked with precision from sourcing to delivery.

Integration with Kitting

SLP CONNECT™ provides real-time visibility into kit components, assembly workflows, and quality control checkpoints. This ensures accuracy, reduces waste, and maintains FDA-aligned compliance across all kitting operations.

Integration with 3PL

For fulfillment and logistics, SLP CONNECT™ synchronizes inventory levels, order processing, and shipment tracking. It enables seamless coordination between warehousing, distribution, and end‑user delivery, ensuring every order moves through the supply chain without blind spots.

Why It Matters

By integrating kitting and 3PL operations, SLP CONNECT™ eliminates delays, reduces errors, and ensures every program runs with clarity, accountability, and operational efficiency. It becomes the connective tissue that keeps all moving parts aligned.
