How Does SLP CONNECT Integrate with Kitting and 3PL?

SLP CONNECT™ is the operational intelligence layer that ties together kitting, fulfillment, inventory, and logistics into one unified system. It ensures every kit, component, and shipment is tracked with precision from sourcing to delivery.

Integration with Kitting

SLP CONNECT™ provides real-time visibility into kit components, assembly workflows, and quality control checkpoints. This ensures accuracy, reduces waste, and maintains FDA-aligned compliance.

Integration with 3PL

For fulfillment and logistics, SLP CONNECT™ synchronizes inventory levels, order processing, and shipment tracking. It enables seamless coordination between warehousing, distribution, and end‑user delivery.

Why It Matters

By integrating kitting and 3PL operations, SLP CONNECT™ eliminates blind spots, reduces delays, and ensures every program runs with clarity, accountability, and operational efficiency.
