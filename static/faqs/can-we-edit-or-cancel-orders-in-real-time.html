Can we edit or cancel orders in real time?

Order management in time-sensitive medical fulfillment programs demands the ability to intervene — modifying or canceling orders before they enter the pick-pack-ship cycle. Strategic Lab Partners exposes order editing and cancellation controls through SLP CONNECT, giving authorized program personnel a defined window to correct errors, update quantities, or stop shipments before they reach the carrier.

Yes. Orders can be edited or cancelled in real time as long as they have not yet been released to fulfillment. This includes address corrections, shipment holds, and full cancellations. These changes can be made through agreed-upon workflows or API integrations, ensuring that only valid, up‑to‑date orders move into picking and packing.

Practical applications:

  • Modifying order quantities or kit configurations in SLP CONNECT before the fulfillment cutoff window closes.
  • Canceling individual orders or batch runs when program enrollment changes or site delays occur.
  • Updating shipping addresses for participant-direct deliveries without re-entering the full order.
  • Receiving automated alerts in SLP CONNECT when an edited order re-enters the fulfillment queue.
  • Maintaining a complete edit and cancellation audit trail for compliance review and billing reconciliation.

Once an order passes a defined release point—such as being picked, packed, or handed to the carrier—changes may require a different process. During onboarding, we work with you to define clear cut‑offs and exception handling rules so your team always knows what is possible at each stage.

What this prevents: delays, loss without defensible history, inventory gaps, and after-the-fact reconciliation.

SLP Advantage: Real‑time control reduces waste, improves accuracy, and protects the customer experience across your entire program.
