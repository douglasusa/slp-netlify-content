What is SLP CONNECT™?

SLP CONNECT™ is Strategic Lab Partners’ operational intelligence platform — the system that unifies kitting, 3PL, inventory, and deployment workflows into a single digital environment. It ensures every component, kit, and shipment is tracked with precision from sourcing to delivery.

The Purpose of SLP CONNECT™

The platform eliminates blind spots by connecting data across sourcing, assembly, fulfillment, and logistics. It provides real‑time visibility, reduces errors, and ensures FDA‑aligned compliance throughout the entire supply chain.

What It Enables

SLP CONNECT™ supports:

  • Real‑time inventory tracking
  • Kit component validation
  • 3PL synchronization
  • Deployment and fulfillment automation
  • Program‑level reporting and oversight

By centralizing operational intelligence, SLP CONNECT™ ensures every program runs with clarity, accountability, and efficiency.

MISSION | Strategic Lab Partners (SLP)
