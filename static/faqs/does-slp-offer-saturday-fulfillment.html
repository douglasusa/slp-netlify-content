Does SLP offer Saturday fulfillment?

Clinical programs that operate on a Monday-through-Friday fulfillment cycle frequently encounter participant scheduling conflicts, holiday disruptions, and enrollment surges that demand weekend shipping capability. Strategic Lab Partners offers Saturday fulfillment for qualifying programs, extending outbound dispatch through SLP CONNECT-coordinated weekend operations to maintain delivery windows without forcing programs to buffer excess inventory.

SLP does not currently offer Saturday fulfillment as a standard service. Most customers have not seen enough operational or clinical value to justify the additional overtime, staffing, and carrier costs associated with weekend operations.

Practical applications:

  • Scheduling Saturday outbound runs for time-sensitive diagnostic kit deliveries to participants or sites.
  • Aligning Saturday fulfillment windows with carrier pickup schedules for guaranteed Monday delivery.
  • Managing clinical trial enrollment surges that spike kit demand across the weekend shipping cycle.
  • Providing SLP CONNECT order status updates for Saturday dispatches identical to weekday operations.
  • Coordinating Saturday receiving for inbound component deliveries from supplier networks with weekend logistics.

That said, we are open to implementing Saturday fulfillment when a program’s volume, urgency, and economics support it. If weekend operations are important to your workflow, we will work with you to model demand, evaluate cost, and design a staffing and carrier strategy that aligns with your program’s needs.

Our goal is to ensure that any service level we provide delivers meaningful value without introducing unnecessary cost or operational complexity.

What this prevents: delays, loss without defensible history, inventory gaps, and after-the-fact reconciliation.

SLP Advantage: You get a data‑driven, transparent assessment of when weekend fulfillment truly benefits your program.

If your program is decentralized or regulated and you're experiencing avoidable disruption, SLP can help you map failure points and implement a more governable approach.
