How does SLP CONNECT™ improve visibility?

Supply chain visibility gaps are the root cause of most program exceptions in medical logistics — when stakeholders cannot see inventory levels, shipment status, or exception conditions in real time, problems compound before they can be addressed. SLP CONNECT resolves this by providing a unified, real-time data layer across SLP's kitting and fulfillment operations, giving program managers the intelligence they need to intervene before exceptions become failures.

Visibility features include:

Practical applications:

  • Accessing live inventory balances at the SKU and lot level through the SLP CONNECT dashboard.
  • Tracking individual shipments from dispatch through final delivery without leaving the SLP CONNECT portal.
  • Receiving automated exception alerts when orders miss SLA thresholds or encounter carrier disruptions.
  • Reviewing historical fulfillment performance metrics to identify recurring friction points in program operations.
  • Exporting SLP CONNECT data into program reporting tools for regulatory submissions and sponsor reviews.
  • Order tracking: status, carrier, and delivery confirmation.
  • Inventory dashboards: kit and component levels updated in real time.
  • Usage insights: which sites or channels are consuming inventory fastest.
  • Program alerts: low stock, backorders, or exceptions.

What this prevents: delays, loss without defensible history, inventory gaps, and after-the-fact reconciliation.

This level of visibility helps teams plan better, reduce risk, and maintain control over program performance.

If your program is decentralized or regulated and you're experiencing avoidable disruption, SLP can help you map failure points and implement a more governable approach.
