What is Strategic Lab Partners?

Strategic Lab Partners (SLP) is a logistics, kitting, and operational support provider serving healthcare training programs, institutions, and organizations that require precision, consistency, and scalable fulfillment.

SLP specializes in healthcare‑grade kitting, FDA‑aligned component management, inventory oversight, and program‑level operational support. Our systems ensure accuracy, traceability, and reliability across every stage of the supply chain.

We support organizations that need dependable fulfillment, compliant workflows, and a partner capable of scaling with program growth.
