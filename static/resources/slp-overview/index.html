SLP Overview

Strategic Lab Partners (SLP) provides regulated kitting, medical fulfillment, and operational oversight for complex, decentralized programs across healthcare, diagnostics, and clinical research.

What SLP delivers:

  • Protocol-to-kit translation and controlled kit assembly
  • FDA-registered fulfillment and temperature-aware logistics workflows
  • Multi-site deployment and reverse logistics designed to reduce loss and rework
  • SLP CONNECT™  the ERP complementing platform truth layer transforms item-level logistics activity into trusted operational intelligence for greater visibility, exception oversight, and audit-ready documentation

If your operations are experiencing delays, surprises, loss, or runaway logistics cost, SLP can help you regain control by standardizing workflows and making performance measurable across every handoff.
