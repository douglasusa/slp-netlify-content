Strategic Lab Partners

Introducing SLP CONNECT™

Enterprise Operational Truth Layer for Healthcare Supply Chain Visibility & Traceability

Purpose-built for decentralized fulfillment in complex, critical, and highly regulated healthcare operations.

SLP CONNECT™ is the Enterprise Operational Truth Layer for Healthcare Supply Chain Visibility and Traceability — an ERP-Complementary Platform (ECP) that transforms real-time, item-level logistics activity into trusted operational intelligence across kitting, fulfillment, delivery, returns, reporting, compliance, and customer-facing transparency.

A Purpose-Built Innovation

SLP CONNECT™ was not built as a generic portal, dashboard, shipment tracker, or ERP replacement. It was purpose-built to control and manage decentralized healthcare fulfillment where operational failure can affect specimen integrity, patient experience, research timelines, regulatory defensibility, cost, and revenue.

Strategic Lab Partners (SLP) provides the healthcare-specific infrastructure that clinical, consumer, research, genomics, diagnostics, precision medicine, and decentralized programs rely on for compliant, scalable, and scientifically reliable operations. Through SLP CONNECT™, SLP unifies precision kitting, regulated fulfillment, 3PL logistics, serialized tracking, real-time visibility, and audit-ready accountability into one governed operational ecosystem.

Strategic Lab Partners Value Proposition

Strategic Lab Partners transforms research infrastructures across genomics, multi-omics, diagnostics, decentralized trials, and precision medicine by unifying data precision, logistical speed, and sample integrity in one connected ecosystem.

Through SLP CONNECT™, its Premium Kitting Deployment and Centralized Management Platform, SLP integrates precision kit production, regulated fulfillment, global and domestic logistics coordination, and real-time digital visibility to ensure complete control from design to deployment.

SLP elevates healthcare supply chains with universal kitting, serialized tracking, and value-based logistics. This strengthens quality, reduces variability, protects specimen integrity, improves operational predictability, and accelerates scientific discovery.

Origin and Reason for Being

SLP CONNECT™ came into being because decentralized healthcare fulfillment had outgrown disconnected tools, spreadsheets, generic logistics systems, and fragmented reporting. Complex healthcare programs require more than the ability to ship kits. They require reliable control over kit design, component sourcing, inventory, assembly, fulfillment, carrier movement, site deployment, at-home collection logistics, specimen returns, chain of custody, exception handling, documentation, and customer communication.

Operating Layer Scope

SLP CONNECT™ does not replace ERP. It improves, interacts with, and complements ERP by capturing the real-time operational activity that determines whether fulfillment is actually reliable, compliant, visible, and customer-ready.

Enterprise Explanation: ERP tells the organization what should be recorded. SLP CONNECT™ shows what is actually happening across the physical healthcare supply chain — in real time, at item level, and with audit-ready evidence.

Differentiation from Innovation

Unlike incumbent ERP, SCM, WMS, TMS, QMS, lab, clinical, middleware, and portal tools that often depend on reconciliation, customization, middleware, or ecosystem lock-in, SLP CONNECT™ consolidates healthcare fulfillment activity into one operational truth layer.

  • Healthcare-specific infrastructure for regulated kitting, fulfillment, deployment, and specimen-related logistics.
  • Validated real-world proof from SLP CONNECT™ deployments in decentralized healthcare logistics.
  • Custom serialization across kits, components, shipments, specimens, returns, custody events, and transactions.
  • Vendor-agnostic architecture that complements existing ERP and enterprise systems.
  • Integrated quality and compliance workflows within the operational transaction layer.
  • Customer-facing visibility that goes beyond internal dashboarding.
