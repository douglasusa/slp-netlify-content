Kitting and Supplies

Kitting and supplies are often treated as transactional commodities, but in decentralized and regulated programs they function as core infrastructure. Every component—tubes, labels, reagents, packaging, documentation, and digital identifiers—must work together as a governed system to ensure accuracy, compliance, and repeatability across every site and every participant. Strategic Lab Partners (SLP) elevates kitting from a procurement activity to an operational discipline, engineered to reduce exceptions and protect chain‑of‑custody at scale.

Through SLP CONNECT™, our integrated ecosystem for precision kit production, serialized tracking, inventory control, and logistics orchestration, sponsors and CROs gain the ability to standardize complexity across multi‑site and multi‑phase programs. This article explores why kitting and supplies are strategic infrastructure—and how SLP’s governed approach stabilizes fulfillment performance, eliminates variability, and enables programs to scale with manufacturing‑grade reliability.
