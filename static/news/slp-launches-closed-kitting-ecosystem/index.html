SLP Introduces Closed Kitting Ecosystem for Real-Time Component-Level Control

June 2026 • Strategic Lab Partners

Strategic Lab Partners (SLP) today introduced a fully closed, healthcare-grade kitting ecosystem designed to give research sponsors, CROs, and clinical operations teams real-time, component-level visibility across every kit they deploy. Built on the SLP CONNECT™ platform, the ecosystem links precision kit production, global logistics, and digital tracking into a single, controlled environment—from design through deployment and return.

Traditional kitting workflows lose visibility the moment a kit leaves the assembly floor. Components are tracked at the kit level, if at all, and chain-of-custody is reconstructed after the fact from paper records and disconnected systems. SLP's closed ecosystem replaces that model with continuous, component-level control, so every device, reagent, and consumable inside a kit is accounted for throughout its lifecycle.

Real-time component-level visibility

Each component is serialized and registered as it enters the kit, allowing teams to see exactly what is in every kit, where it is, and what condition it is in. This granularity supports faster issue resolution, tighter inventory accuracy, and proactive replenishment before shortages affect study timelines.

Digital chain-of-custody

The ecosystem maintains a continuous digital chain-of-custody for every kit and its contents, capturing handoffs across production, logistics, and field use. This creates an auditable record that strengthens compliance, reduces protocol deviations, and gives sponsors confidence in sample integrity from collection through transport.

A closed, healthcare-grade environment

By keeping kitting within a closed system, SLP reduces variability and removes the gaps where errors and uncertainty typically enter the supply chain. The result is a scalable, compliant operation that helps research teams run complex studies with advanced-manufacturing accuracy.

The closed kitting ecosystem is available now through SLP CONNECT™. To learn how it can support your program, contact Strategic Lab Partners at +1 800-985-3422.
