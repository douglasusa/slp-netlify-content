SLP Introduces Closed Kitting Ecosystem for Real-Time Component-Level Control
June 2026 • Strategic Lab Partners

Strategic Lab Partners (SLP) has launched a fully closed, healthcare‑grade kitting ecosystem designed to give laboratories, healthcare organizations, and high‑volume fulfillment operations unprecedented real‑time visibility into every component inside every kit. The new system integrates sterile‑environment assembly, digital chain‑of‑custody, and automated component‑level tracking into a single operational workflow.

Healthcare‑Grade Precision at Scale

The ecosystem is built around SLP’s FDA‑registered kitting infrastructure, which has produced more than 55 million kits across clinical, retail, and institutional markets. Each kit is assembled in a controlled environment using sterile‑gloved technicians, stainless workstations, and validated component bins to ensure consistency and compliance.

Components such as sterile swabs, saliva or blood‑spot tubes, lancets, alcohol prep pads, biohazard bags, absorbent pads, instruction cards, and prepaid return mailers are tracked from inbound receipt through final packaging.

Real-Time Component-Level Control

The new closed ecosystem integrates directly with SLP CONNECT™, enabling real‑time visibility into component usage, lot numbers, expiration dates, and kit‑level assembly status. This ensures complete chain‑of‑custody clarity and eliminates blind spots common in traditional fulfillment workflows.

Designed for Laboratories, Retailers, and Institutional Partners

The system supports direct‑to‑consumer (DTC) programs, clinical trial logistics, retail health initiatives, and institutional testing workflows. With configurable assembly lines and digital overlays, partners can scale production while maintaining strict compliance and operational transparency.

About Strategic Lab Partners

Strategic Lab Partners is a leading provider of FDA‑registered healthcare kitting, regulated fulfillment, and operational intelligence solutions. Serving more than 400 clients nationwide, SLP delivers end‑to‑end visibility, precision, and reliability across every stage of the kitting lifecycle.
